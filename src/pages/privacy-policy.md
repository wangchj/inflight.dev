# Privacy Policy

**Effective**: July 1, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal
information or data you use within the app.

## Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any of your request data, environment configurations, or any
other information you enter in the app.

This app does not require you to create an account. All data is stored locally on your device and
remains private to you.

## No Analytics or Tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking tools, or third-party services to collect information about
your usage.

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Check this page for updates.

By using Inflight, you consent to this Privacy Policy.
